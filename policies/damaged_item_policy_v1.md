# Damaged Item Policy

Version: 1.0 | Effective: 2026-01-01  
**Document ID:** damaged_item_policy_v1

## Overview

Customers can report damaged items within 7 days of delivery. Every damaged-item claim requires photo evidence and reviewer review.

## Eligibility Conditions

The damage report must be submitted within 7 days of the delivery date.
Photo evidence of the damage is required.
Every damaged-item claim is reviewed by a human reviewer.

## Process

1. Record the delivery date, report date, and description of the damage.
2. Request and attach a photo of the damaged item.
3. Escalate every case to a human reviewer.
4. The reviewer assesses the evidence and determines the appropriate resolution.

Available resolution options are a replacement, a refund, or store credit. A refund requires reviewer approval.

## Exceptions

Reports made more than 7 days after delivery fall outside this policy's reporting window and must be referred to a human reviewer for assessment. Missing photo evidence should be requested; the case remains subject to human review.
