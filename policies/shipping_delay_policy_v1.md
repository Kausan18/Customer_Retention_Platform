# Shipping Delay Policy

Version: 1.0 | Effective: 2026-01-01  
**Document ID:** shipping_delay_policy_v1

## Overview

A shipping delay is recorded when an order is more than 3 days past its estimated delivery date. The agent may provide a status update or escalate the delay, but compensation is not automatic.

## Eligibility Conditions

A delay is counted when delivery is more than 3 days past the estimated delivery date.
The agent may provide a status update to the customer.
Compensation decisions are escalated to a human reviewer.

## Process

1. Check the estimated delivery date and the latest available shipment status.
2. The agent may provide the customer with a status update.
3. The agent may escalate the delay for further review.

## Exceptions

- No compensation is automatic under this policy.
- Requests for compensation or other exceptions must be escalated to a human reviewer.
