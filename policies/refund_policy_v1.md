# Refund Policy

Version: 1.0 | Effective: 2026-01-01  
**Document ID:** refund_policy_v1

## Overview

The refund window is 30 days from delivery. Every refund requires inspection to pass and reviewer approval before money is returned.

## Eligibility Conditions

The refund window is 30 days from the delivery date.
The return must be received by the store.
Inspection must pass.
A reviewer must approve every refund.
The order must not already be fully refunded.

## Process

1. The customer requests a refund and returns the item.
2. The return receipt is recorded.
3. The item is inspected.
4. A reviewer evaluates the request and approves or declines the refund.
5. The customer is informed of the review outcome. A refund is not issued before reviewer approval.

## Exceptions

- Damaged items are handled separately under the damaged item policy.
- Suspected fraud is escalated to a human reviewer.
