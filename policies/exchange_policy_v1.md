# Exchange Policy

Version: 1.0 | Effective: 2026-01-01  
**Document ID:** exchange_policy_v1

## Overview

The exchange window is 15 days from delivery. Exchanges are limited to items in the same product category.

## Eligibility Conditions

The exchange window is 15 days from the delivery date.
The replacement item must be in the same category as the original.
The replacement item must be in stock.
A reviewer must approve every exchange.
The original item must be returned.

## Process

1. The customer requests an exchange and identifies the replacement item.
2. The replacement's category and stock availability are checked.
3. The customer returns the original item.
4. The exchange is processed after the original item is received and the replacement's availability is confirmed.

## Exceptions

- An exchange is an item-for-item resolution, not a cash transaction.
- If the replacement costs more than the original item, no cash difference is collected; the customer may proceed with the exchange only.
- If a suitable replacement is not in stock, the exchange cannot be completed while it is unavailable.
